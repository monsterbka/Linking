## **Notes per claim**

The contemporary landscape for independent software vendors and individual game developers operating globally from Vietnam represents a highly complex matrix of technical compliance, platform economics, cross-border taxation, and stringent domestic regulatory frameworks. A comprehensive analysis of the claims surrounding Apple, Google, Microsoft, the United States Internal Revenue Service (IRS), and the Vietnamese Government reveals the structural realities dictating the operational viability of a sole proprietor in 2026\.

### **Ecosystem Asset Specifications and Merchandising Mechanics (Claims 1, 2, 3, 6, 13\)**

The visual compliance requirements enforced by major digital storefronts function as the primary gating mechanism for algorithmic discoverability and initial user acquisition. Technical deviations from these strict asset topologies invariably lead to manual review rejections or severe algorithmic suppression.

#### **Apple App Store Topologies**

Apple’s hardware fragmentation, driven by iterative expansions in display dimensions, requires exhaustive asset generation. The introduction of the 6.9-inch display class—accommodating devices such as the iPhone 16 Pro Max, 17 Pro Max, and 18 Pro Max—mandates precise rendering. The developer must upload assets targeting one of the following resolutions: 1320x2868, 1290x2796, or 1260x27361. The system permits between one and ten screenshots per localization, highlighting the operational burden of translating and rendering assets for a global launch1.  
Similarly, scaling algorithms dictate that if specific tablet assets are absent, the platform will utilize scaled variants of the next largest display1. However, providing native 13-inch iPad specifications (2064x2752 or 2048x2732) is explicitly required if the binary is flagged to run on iPad OS1.

| Apple Device Class | Accepted Portrait Resolutions (Pixels) | Accepted Landscape Resolutions (Pixels) |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **iPhone 6.9"** | 1320x2868, 1290x2796, 1260x2736 | 2868x1320, 2796x1290, 2736x1260 |
| **iPad 13"** | 2064x2752, 2048x2732 | 2752x2064, 2732x2048 |
| **App Icon** | 1024x1024 (No Alpha) | N/A |

The strict prohibition of alpha channels (transparency) in the 1024x1024 PNG app icon is a foundational UI rule2. Apple’s operating systems dynamically apply corner radii (the signature "squircle") and, since iOS 18, perform algorithmic color tinting for Dark Mode aesthetics. Pre-masked or transparent icons interfere with these system-level rendering pipelines, resulting in immediate rejection.  
For dynamic merchandising, App Previews are constrained to a minimum of 15 seconds and a maximum of 30 seconds1. Developers may upload up to three previews per localization1. Because these videos auto-play in the search results and replace the first screenshot, the default 5-second poster frame requires deliberate curation to maximize conversion rates.

#### **Google Play Visual Merchandising**

Google Play’s visual taxonomy prioritizes a unified header asset known as the Feature Graphic over device-specific screenshot perfection. This asset must be precisely 1024x500 pixels (JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no alpha)5. The composition of this graphic is heavily policed; developers must avoid placing brand logos or primary text in the "cutoff zones" near the edges, as the graphic dynamically scales across Android TV, Chromebooks, and automotive displays5.  
While developers can upload up to eight screenshots per device, the claim that tablet screenshots are "optional" is fundamentally misleading. While an app can be published without them, Google Play’s recommendation algorithms actively suppress titles from premium promotional slots unless the developer provides a minimum of four screenshots for both 7-inch and 10-inch form factors, with at least four standard screenshots achieving a resolution of 1080px or higher5. This reflects Google's strategic push into the large-screen market.

#### **Microsoft Store Cinematic Formatting**

Microsoft approaches store design through a cinematic, console-first lens, attempting to differentiate itself from the utilitarian grids of mobile stores. Game listing art requires specific aspect ratios mirroring traditional media packaging: a 2:3 Poster Art (720x1080 or 1440x2160), a 1:1 Box Art (1080x1080 or 2160x2160), and a massive 16:9 Super Hero Art asset (1920x1080 or 3840x2160) for premium carousel placement6.

### **Platform Distribution Policies, Economics, and Lifecycle Friction (Claims 7, 10, 11, 12\)**

The economic and operational friction of maintaining software distribution varies drastically across platforms.

#### **Microsoft's Aggressive Developer Acquisition**

In an effort to court independent developers away from the iOS and Android duopoly, Microsoft has engineered a highly frictionless economic environment. The standard revenue share sits at a competitive 12% for games and 15% for standard applications8. Furthermore, Microsoft has waived the historical \$19 registration fee for individual developer accounts and, according to documentation detailing the new onboarding flow, has also waived the \$99 company registration fee10. This zero-friction financial entry makes it a highly attractive secondary market for an individual developer.

#### **Google Play Testing Mandates**

Google’s policy regarding pre-launch testing represents a significant operational barrier for independent creators. For all personal developer accounts created after November 13, 2023, Google initially instituted a draconian requirement: developers had to secure 20 opted-in testers for a minimum of 14 continuous days before being granted production access13.  
This mandate was designed to crowdsource Quality Assurance (QA) and artificially throttle the influx of low-effort software and malware. However, it placed an extraordinary burden on solo developers lacking established communities. In a rare concession to intense developer feedback, Google amended this policy in December 2024, reducing the requirement from 20 testers down to 12, though the 14-day continuous opt-in period remains strictly enforced17.

#### **Android SDK Migration Cycles**

Software maintenance is a perpetual obligation. Google enforces an aggressive API target lifecycle to ensure applications conform to modern security and memory management standards. New applications and updates must target Android 16 (API level 36\) by August 31, 202618. Developers who fail to migrate their codebases will find their applications suppressed or entirely hidden from users operating on newer hardware. While an extension mechanism exists—allowing developers to delay compliance until November 1, 2026—the structural reality is that digital software requires continuous engineering expenditure18.

### **Cross-Border Taxation and US Withholding Asymmetries (Claims 5, 9, 17\)**

For an individual developer residing in Vietnam and publishing globally, the divergence in how Apple and Google classify digital proceeds dictates their ultimate profit margins. This divergence is rooted in the United States Internal Revenue Code (IRC) Chapter 3, which mandates a 30% withholding tax on US-sourced income paid to foreign persons, specifically targeting passive income such as copyright royalties.

#### **The Apple Commission Model**

Apple structures its App Store Developer Agreement strictly as a "sales and commission" arrangement20. Apple acts as the Merchant of Record (MoR). From a legal standpoint, the end-user purchases the software directly from Apple, and Apple subsequently remits a commission to the developer. The IRS does not classify these active sales proceeds as royalties. Consequently, Apple explicitly does not withhold the 30% US tax on standard App Store sales for non-U.S. entities20. This structure protects the Vietnamese developer's gross margins on iOS.

#### **The Google Royalty Model**

Conversely, Google's legal architecture categorizes developer payouts—particularly advertising revenue from AdMob and specific Google Play disbursements—as "Other Copyright Royalties"24. Because royalties are subject to Chapter 3 withholding, Google is legally obligated to enforce a 30% deduction on any revenue generated from users located within the United States25.

#### **The US-Vietnam Tax Treaty Void**

In international tax law, a bilateral double taxation treaty can override Chapter 3, reducing the 30% withholding rate to a fraction (often 15%, 10%, or 0%). A comprehensive income tax treaty between the United States and Vietnam was signed in 2015\. However, signing a treaty does not enact it; it requires ratification by the US Senate. As of 2026, the Senate has not ratified this treaty, rendering it legally void21.  
The Internal Revenue Manual acknowledges Vietnam's status but confirms no active treaty benefits apply to the current unified state26. Therefore, an individual developer in Vietnam bears the absolute maximum 30% withholding on all US-sourced Google Play and AdMob royalty revenue, a massive operational disadvantage compared to the iOS ecosystem.

### **Vietnam Domestic Regulation: The Ministry Merger and Licensing (Claims 4, 8, 14, 15\)**

Vietnam operates a highly regulated domestic digital ecosystem, which underwent massive structural consolidation between 2024 and 2026\.

#### **The Ministerial Consolidation**

On March 1, 2025, executing the directives of Resolution 57 (Nghị quyết 57), the Vietnamese government merged the Ministry of Information and Communications (MIC) and the Ministry of Science and Technology (MOST). This formed the new **Ministry of Science, Technology and Communications** (Bộ Khoa học, Công nghệ và Truyền thông)27. This super-ministry was designed to eliminate bureaucratic overlap between digital infrastructure management, state media control, and technological research and development31.

#### **Decree 147/2024/ND-CP and Platform Enforcement**

Effective December 25, 2024, Decree 147/2024/ND-CP overhauled the management of internet services and online information, including video games33. Both Apple and Google enforce this strictly at the platform level. Since February 4, 2025, Apple requires developers to explicitly input their Vietnamese game license number and associated URL into App Store Connect to maintain visibility on the Vietnam storefront35. Google Play echoes this mandate33.

#### **The G1-G4 Classification Conundrum**

The regulatory friction for an individual developer centers on the classification of games (G1 through G4). Vietnamese law stipulates that only legally registered domestic enterprises (companies) can apply for G1 licenses or G2-G4 certificates; individuals are entirely barred from obtaining them.  
The exact classification of a single-player offline puzzle game that serves advertisements (but lacks server-side synchronous gameplay) remains a critical ambiguity in the provided literature. While traditional definitions categorize server-less games as G4, Decree 147's exact handling of localized ad-serving modules is not explicitly defined in the available intelligence. However, the operational reality is stark: Apple and Google employ automated compliance enforcement. Because the developer is an individual and cannot secure a license, the platforms will proactively delist the application from the Vietnamese iOS and Android storefronts to maintain their own compliance with the Ministry of Science, Technology and Communications33. The developer's primary recourse is to geo-block Vietnam and focus exclusively on global distribution.

### **Vietnam Domestic Taxation: Decree 141/2026/ND-CP (Claim 16\)**

While the regulatory environment for game distribution is restrictive, the domestic tax environment for small digital creators underwent a radically positive transformation in 2026\.  
Historically, under Decree 68/2026/ND-CP, individuals and business households earning over 100 million VND annually were subject to Value Added Tax (VAT) and Personal Income Tax (PIT) under a flat presumption model36.  
On April 29, 2026, the government issued **Decree 141/2026/ND-CP**, which took effect on January 1, 2026, applying retroactively to the fiscal year38. This decree dramatically raised the tax-exempt revenue threshold to **1 billion VND per year** (approximately \$40,000 USD)36.

| Vietnam Domestic Tax Decree | Effective Date | Annual Revenue Exemption Threshold | E-Invoice Mandate Trigger |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Decree 68/2026/ND-CP** | Early 2026 | 100 Million VND (Temporary 500M) | \> 500 Million VND |
| **Decree 141/2026/ND-CP** | Jan 1, 2026 | 1 Billion VND | \> 1 Billion VND |

For an individual game developer repatriating proceeds from foreign app stores, revenue up to 1 billion VND annually is now entirely exempt from domestic VAT and PIT36. Furthermore, they are exempt from the burdensome requirement to utilize the state's electronic invoice system (hóa đơn điện tử) until they breach this new threshold37.  
If the developer's revenue exceeds 1 billion VND, they enter a 30-day grace period to register for the e-invoicing system and must transition to standard tax declarations37. While the specific VAT and PIT percentages for software distribution above this threshold are governed by existing tax codes not explicitly outlined in the new decree (which defaults to "current regulations"), the elevation of the threshold represents a deliberate macroeconomic strategy. By absorbing an estimated state revenue reduction of 16.65 trillion VND, the Ministry of Finance and the Ministry of Science, Technology and Communications aim to incubate grassroots digital creators, allowing them to scale without being smothered by early-stage tax liabilities and administrative overhead38.

## **Gaps**

* **Vietnam G4 Classification Nuance for Ad-Supported Offline Games:** The available intelligence strictly confirms that Decree 147/2024/ND-CP regulates game distribution, but it lacks the granular legislative text detailing the exact parameters that separate an unclassified utility app from a G4 video game if it features zero server connections but still serves external advertisements. It remains unverified if removing certain interactive elements bypasses the G4 definition, or if individuals are entirely locked out by Apple and Google's blanket compliance algorithms without exception.  
* **Tax Rates Above 1 Billion VND:** While Decree 141/2026/ND-CP clearly establishes the 1 billion VND exemption threshold, the specific VAT and PIT percentage rates applied to the overflow revenue are not detailed in the provided materials. The texts state that standard declaration follows "current regulations," necessitating further review of overarching Vietnamese personal income tax codes for exact percentage liabilities.
