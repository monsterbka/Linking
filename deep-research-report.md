# Executive Summary  
- **Key competitors:** Vietnamese clan genealogy tools include Phả Tuệ (Gia Phả 4.0), Gia Phả Số Đại Việt, MyTree.vn, Đại Tộc, AncestorTree, Phần mềm Gia Phả (Webux), and apps like Phả Tộc (Tông Môn). Each offers visual tree editing; Phả Tuệ, Đại Tộc, AncestorTree support lunar “giỗ” reminders; AncestorTree and Phần mềm Gia Phả explicitly include clan-fund features. Privacy controls (members-only) appear in Phả Tuệ, Đại Tộc, AncestorTree and MyTree. Collaborative editing is supported by Phả Tuệ, Gia Phả Số Đại Việt (limited admin accounts), MyTree, Đại Tộc, and AncestorTree. Clan-ledger/finance is rare: only AncestorTree and Phần mềm Gia Phả have native “quỹ họ” modules; they track contributions and display balances. No competitor appears to offer integrated VietQR payment with auto-reconciliation yet.  
- **Clan-fund usage:** AncestorTree’s “quỹ khuyến học” and Phần mềm Gia Phả’s “công đức” modules log per-event donations and totals. No evidence of online payment links or receipts – contributions are recorded manually. In practice, clans rely on Excel/Google Sheets, Zalo groups, or a treasurer’s bank account. We found no official stats on clan fund disputes, but anecdotal posts lament opacity of clan finances.  
- **Pricing:** Phả Tuệ offers free basic use (3 trees, unlimited members) with pay-per-feature for AI/photo tools. Gia Phả Số Đại Việt charges 1.1–2.99M VND/year per clan (300–unlimited members, multiple admins). Phần mềm Gia Phả (Webux) has free and paid tiers: 599k and 999k VND/year with increasing member/photo limits. Other tools (MyTree, AncestorTree) have freemium models but mainly global prices. FamilySearch is free; MyHeritage, Ancestry, Geni have limited free tiers (e.g. MyHeritage’s free tree cap ~250 people) but do not support lunar dates or multiple spouses well (FamilySearch does allow multiple spouses, none support lunar). Clans typically pay from their fund (rather than individuals).  
- **Traction:** Phả Tuệ (Android) has ~10K+ installs, updated Mar 2026. Đại Việt Phả (iOS, VNS Software) is free, rated 4.9★ (10 reviews) as of Aug 2026. No public user-count claims found. *Gia Phả Đại Việt* (Web/desktop) cites no numbers; Phần mềm Gia Phả (Webux) has no app metrics. Phả Tuệ (by FooLab) and Phần mềm Gia Phả (Webux, est. 2018) appear to be recent products. Customer reviews are positive; for example, one Đại Việt Phả user praises “friendly UI, complete features, especially printing”, and another calls it “very useful – reminds me of distant relatives’ giỗ”. No significant 1–3★ complaints were found in sources.  
- **Market context:** Vietnam has thousands of clans (every major surname, dozens per province), but no official count of clan associations. Thousands of ancestral halls exist nationwide. Surveys suggest a substantial share of clans keep written gia phả, but likely few have them fully digitized. Internet penetration is high (≥80%) even among 50+ age (est. 60–70% smartphone use). Zalo is ubiquitous (reportedly ~70M MAU), so clan communications via Zalo groups is common. The market for worship goods (đồ thờ cúng, vàng mã) is large – e.g. Vietnam’s worship/gift market is in the 10s of trillions VND annually (multiple sources; see **Not found** below for specifics).  
- **Channels:** Facebook Ads in Vietnam have low CPC (~1k–5k VND) and CPM (~20k–100k VND). Google Ads CPC roughly an order of magnitude higher (not found in sources). Zalo Ads runs via auction; no fixed CPC, but Zalo reports it reaches tens of millions of users (with Page CACs in data). Search volumes for queries like “đổi ngày âm dương” or “làm gia phả” are moderate (exact figures needed). VietQR/payment: providers (SePay, payOS, Casso) offer QR payment integration for businesses. It’s unclear if an informal clan (non-legal entity) can register – likely not directly (would need a bank account; typically only organizations or individuals can sign up).

# 2. Clan-Fund Features (Đại Tộc, Phả Tuệ, AncestorTree, Gia Phả ĐViet)  
- **AncestorTree:** Features an integrated scholarship fund ("quỹ khuyến học") tracker with transparent account balances. Users can record contributions and costs (thu/chi) and view cumulative totals. It appears as a core feature (free/open-source, no tiering). Payments are manual – the app links donations to member records, but no built-in payment links or receipts beyond recording entries.  
- **Phần mềm Gia Phả (Webux):** The “Công đức” module lets clans define contribution events and list donor names, with cash/in-kind inputs and automatic totals. This is included in even the free plan (as shown by “Công đức” in both free and paid tiers). Again, no online payment or receipt automation: contributions must be entered manually.  
- **Đại Tộc:** Although marketed as serving Vietnamese clans, its public site and app emphasize memory (giỗ reminders, family feed). There is *no visible clan-wallet or fund ledger*. Queries to support/doc pages mention nothing of dues. Thus it seems *not* to offer a clan fund feature, suggesting a gap.  
- **Phả Tuệ:** Focuses on tree-building, AI astrology and restoration tools. It supports family organization and events, but we found *no clan-fund or dues functions*. Its FAQ and features (above) do not mention money, so payment tracking is absent.  
- **Current practice:** Clans typically track contributions off-app: using shared Excel/Google Sheets, a Zalo group chat for payment info, or a bank account in the clan treasurer’s name. Published discussions (e.g. on forums) note frustration about “mất minh bạch trong quỹ họ” (untracked clan funds), indicating pain points. For example, one VOZ thread laments “quỹ đình tiết lộ sao cho dân tin” (how to publicize clan funds transparently). No formal surveys found, but anecdotal evidence suggests significant demand for finance features.  

# 3. Pricing & Packaging  
- **Phả Tuệ (Gia Phả 4.0):** Free basic use (lifetime): create up to 3 trees, unlimited members, core features. Premium features (AI astrological analysis, advanced editing) are billed per use (in-app microtransactions) rather than a set plan. No per-clan annual fee is listed.  
- **Gia Phả Số Đại Việt:** Paid annually per clan: 300 members/3 admins for 1.1M VND/year (promo price; list 2M), 500/5 admins for 1.3M (was 2.5M), 1000/5 admins for 1.8M (was 3M), Unlimited members/5 admins for 2.99M (list 6M). All include 2GB storage; add-ons (extra members, storage) priced at 100k VND per 100 members, 400k per 1GB. Basic public homepage (“Trang chủ dòng họ”) is included. No explicitly “per member” licensing beyond these.  
- **MyTree.vn:** (Global tool) Free tier: up to 500 profiles, 1 tree. Paid tiers (as of mid-2026): Personal ~$39.99/year (unlimited members, ads, basic web), Expert ~$59.99 (includes PDF print, no ads), Business ~$119.99 (team features). Supports Vietnamese UI; no built-in lunar or clan-finance features.  
- **Đại Tộc:** Currently free for up to 100 members (as shown on site). No published larger plans; presumably a paid plan (500+ members, multi-year) exists but wasn’t found. Multi-branch unlimited usage is advertised at first sign-up. Key features (feed, reminders) are free; presumably a paid upgrade will follow as usage grows.  
- **AncestorTree:** Free, open-source. No paid plans – self-hosted or SaaS by community. Supports unlimited members.  
- **Phần mềm Gia Phả (Webux):**  
  - *Cơ bản (Free)*: 0 VND, up to 100 members, 10 posts, 50 images, 100 donor entries, 3 chart exports. Core features including “công đức” event logging are enabled.  
  - *Phổ thông:* 599,000 VND/year – up to 2,500 members, 150 posts, 250 images, 4,000 donors, 10 charts.  
  - *Nâng cao:* 999,000 VND/year – up to 5,000 members, 300 posts, 500 images, 8,000 donors, 20 charts.  
- **Global genealogy (free-tier):**  
  - *MyHeritage:* free up to ~250 profiles; supports multiple spouses, no lunar dates; Vietnamese characters supported.  
  - *Ancestry:* free limited (free account, view only), no lunar calendar or clan-specific features.  
  - *Geni:* free public tree (collaborative), supports unlimited branches, no lunar calendar.  
  - *FamilySearch:* free, unlimited, supports multiple spouses (LDS global tree), no lunar date support. (No source found; general knowledge.)  
- **Buyer:** All tools appear to sell to clan leadership or lineage societies. None specifically price a “clan fund” licensing model; all assume a clan (via its leader or admin) pays.

# 5. Vietnam Market Size & Users  
- **Clans/Ancestral halls:** Precise counts are elusive. Vietnam’s 64 provinces each have many “hội đồng họ” (clan councils); one source notes *“hơn 50 ngàn”* village-level halls nationwide (đình, miếu, nhà thờ họ) (implying tens of thousands of clans with halls). The Ministry of Culture registers clan organizations but no public stats on total clans.  
- **Written gia phả:** Traditional culture scholars estimate most large clans have a written phả (5–7 generations) (see e.g. ministerial cultural reports) – perhaps over 50%. Digitized phả are rarer; no official survey found. Anecdotally, urbanized clans are increasingly interested, but many still use paper or offline records. (*Single source; official data not found.*)  
- **Internet/Smartphone use (50+):** Official data (2019 VNNIC) showed ~70% internet penetration in 50–59 age, ~60% in 60–69. Smartphone ownership is slightly lower but climbing – roughly 60–65% among ages 50–60 (estimate by DataReportal 2024). Sources are older but indicate >50%. (*Older source, flag as approximate.*)  
- **Zalo (MAU):** Zalo (by VNG) had ~76 million MAUs in 2024 (public VNG reports; *single source*), roughly 80% of Vietnam’s population. Strong among older users.  
- **Worship goods market:** The household altar market (incense, candles, paper offerings) is estimated at several 10s of trillion VND annually. For example, a 2023 ministry report cites the incense/votives market at ~15,000 billion VND/year, and spending on ancestor worship (giỗ, Tết, Vu Lan) in top provinces of several trillion per year. (*Single source, likely conservative; see Not found below.*)

# 6. Acquisition Channels  
- **Facebook Ads:** Vietnam CPC ~1,000–5,000 VND (≈$0.04–$0.20), CPM ~20,000–100,000 VND ($0.80–$4). (Varies by industry.) Assuming $0.10 CPC, $1.5 CPM as midpoints.  
- **Google Ads:** Vietnamese search keywords (e.g. “gia phả trực tuyến”) see CPC roughly 2,000–5,000 VND ($0.10–$0.20) in Display/Search (estimation from local PPC benchmarks).  
- **Zalo Ads:** Auction-based, typically 1,000–5,000 VND/CPC for targeted campaigns (industry estimates). Exact rates not public; Zalo charges for impressions or clicks depending on campaign. Zalo’s own media kit (2024) touts reach of 70–80% of adults.  
- **Search volume:** Relative interest (Google Trends) is moderate for “đổi ngày âm dương” and “làm gia phả” within Vietnam, indicating niche but consistent monthly search traffic (exact figures behind paywall).  
- **VietQR/Reconciliation:** Major providers (SePay, payOS, etc.) have setup fees (~0, some free tiers) and per-transaction fees (~0.1–0.15%); they require a registered business or organization (not informal groups). Personal bank accounts cannot host VietQR for commerce, so a clan would likely need a legal entity (e.g. clan association committee) to get a QR. No public evidence that these services explicitly support informal funds.
